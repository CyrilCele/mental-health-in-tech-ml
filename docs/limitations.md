# Limitations

This document will be completed as the analysis progresses.

Known areas that must be considered include:

- survey representativeness
- missing data
- response bias
- demographic representation
- sensitive mental-health information
- interpretation of self-reported responses
- correlation versus causation
- clustering uncertainty
- sensitivity to preprocessing choices
- sensitivity to model parameters
- potential misuse of respondent segmentation

Cluster membership must not be interpreted as a clinical diagnosis or as a basis for making individual employment decisions.

# Methodology

## Analytical Framework

This project follows the CRISP-DM framework:

1. Business Understanding
2. Data Understanding
3. Data Preparation
4. Modeling
5. Evaluation
6. Communication and Reproducibility

## Machine-Learning Scope

The machine-learning methods used in this project are restricted to techniques covered by the course for this module.

The planned clustering methods are:

- K-Means
- Gaussian Mixture Models
- Agglomerative Hierarchical Clustering
- DBSCAN

The planned dimensionality-reduction methods are:

- Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
- Multidimensional Scaling (MDS)
- Locally Linear Embedding (LLE), where justified by the observed data structure

The final selection of preprocessing, feature engineering, feature selection, dimensionality reduction, clustering parameters, and clustering approach will be determined from the evidence produced during the analysis.

No model will be selected before the relevant experiments have been completed.

## Interpretation

Clusters represent statistical patterns among survey respondents.

They are not clinical diagnoses and must not be interpreted as medical or psychiatric classifications.
